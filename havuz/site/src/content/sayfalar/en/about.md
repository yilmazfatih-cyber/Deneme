Sedir was born in 2021 in the back room of a hair salon. Our co-founder Elif spent an hour a day taking bookings by phone, and another hour worrying about the chairs left empty by customers who did not show up. The booking software on the market was either too complicated or built for big chains.

Today Sedir simplifies bookings for small businesses, from hair salons to dental clinics, from pilates studios to consultancies.

## Our principles

- **Simple wins.** If someone using a feature for the first time cannot understand it without help, it is not ready.
- **Your customers' data is yours.** You can export your data at any time and close your account in one step.
- **Pricing is transparent.** No hidden fees, no commitments, no automatic upgrades to expensive plans.

## Team

We are a small but experienced team. The person who answers your support request is part of the team that builds the product.
